Our story

We believe the best mornings start with intention.

Northstar started with two friends, a tiny roaster, and a shared obsession with finding coffee that tasted as good as the people behind it.

Today, we work with producers we know, roast in small batches in Portland, and keep our cafés relaxed, welcoming, and a little bit weird.
